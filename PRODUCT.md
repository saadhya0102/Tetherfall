# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Windows desktop, native Unity application.

## Stack

Unity 6000.4.7f1 with the built-in render pipeline and a code-generated 3D runtime world.

## Users

Players seeking an original exploration-led third-person fantasy action RPG with a small human party, voluntary Echosworn partnerships, settlement repair, and a complete offline campaign.

## Product Purpose

Project Tetherfall follows a Wayfinder reconnecting Veyra after the Stillfall. Players explore threatened regions, make voluntary Pacts with Echosworn, recruit human companions, repair Hearthgates, and restore routes between communities.

## Positioning

Tetherfall combines expedition-based action, layered traversal, party support, settlement restoration, and reciprocal creature partnerships. Echosworn have agency and are never framed as captured property or forced labor.

## Operating Context

The playable Unity build is a growing vertical slice. Players use keyboard and mouse in short expeditions and longer story sessions. The canonical product and design source is `..\PROJECT_TETHERFALL_PRD.md`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Original fiction and visual expression only.
- Windows PC first and offline-first solo play.
- Future invite-only 2–4 player co-op is architectural preparation, not an initial feature.
- No gacha, loot boxes, paid power, forced creature capture, forced breeding, or forced labor.
- Use clearly licensed CC0/open assets with local provenance records alongside original authored assets.
- Accessibility, remapping, reduced motion, color-independent feedback, captions, and difficulty support remain core requirements.

## Brand Commitments

- Working title: Project Tetherfall.
- Canonical vocabulary: Veyra, Stillfall, Wayfinder, Threadglass, Echosworn, Pact, Accord, Hearthgate, and Hollow Choir.
- Tone: hopeful frontier fantasy with wonder, responsibility, and visible repair.

## Evidence on Hand

- `..\PROJECT_TETHERFALL_PRD.md` defines the full game direction and vertical-slice scope.
- `Assets\Scripts` contains the current playable code-generated prototype.
- No production title, logo, or commercial claims have legal clearance.

## Product Principles

- Prefer dense, reactive expeditions to empty map scale.
- Reward skill, exploration, and meaningful choices over grinding.
- Keep Echosworn relationships reciprocal.
- Build systems that can accept authored art and content without a rewrite.
- Preserve player agency, accessibility, privacy, and solo completeness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard and mouse controls with a path to controller support and remapping.
- Adjustable camera comfort, UI scale, reduced motion, and color-independent state cues.
- Critical progression remains accessible when assistance is enabled.
